## What's new in 0.3.1

Changes since [0.3.0](https://github.com/x4rd4s/open1602/releases/tag/v0.3.0), most of them from
the second round of player feedback.

### New

- **News of other players' wars:** the status bar reports when two other players go to war or make
  peace, and the help line over a partner's coat of arms in the diplomacy window names whom that
  partner is at war with. A switch in the display window turns the news off.
- **New player colours:** magenta and dark grey replace light blue and lime, which were too close
  to blue, teal and green.
- **Groups** (Ctrl + number) are kept in the savegame and come back after loading.

### Fixes

- Walkers and carts move their legs at every frame rate and zoom level.
- The fire brigade puts out a fire from the road in front of the building instead of vanishing
  into it.
- The water mill's wheel turns in the river instead of standing on land.
- F at a forester plants the trees' variants mixed, as when planting by hand.
